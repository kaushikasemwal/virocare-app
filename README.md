ViroCare — Quarantine & Treatment Facility OperationsViroCare is a lightweight, single-file web application prototype built to coordinate daily patient care, temperature tracking, doctor reviews, discharges, and outcome monitoring for a 74-bed virus quarantine and treatment facility.Problem StatementAn understaffed 74-bed quarantine facility operating at full occupancy relied on paper journals for daily temperature logs, causing:Missed or duplicate temperature measurements.Doctors visiting patients before nurses recorded their daily temperatures.Delayed discharges when patients met the 3 consecutive fever-free days criterion.No visibility into facility mortality rates against the 15% mortality ceiling (85% survival benchmark).Key FeaturesRole-Based Access & Login Portal: Separate workspaces for Doctor, Nurse, and Administrator roles with 1-click demo sign-in.Immediate Check Priority Queue: High-fever patients (≥ 38.0°C by default) and manually flagged urgent cases are pinned to the top of every screen so they receive treatment first.Interactive 74-Bed Ward Map: Color-coded grid across Wing A (A-01–A-10) and Wing B (B-01–B-64) showing bed status at a glance (Immediate/Fever, Needs Temp, Needs Doctor, Discharge Ready, Up to Date, Vacant) with 1-click role actions.Nurse Rapid Round Mode: Bed-by-bed stepper with 1-tap temperature presets and auto-advance to speed up rounds for understaffed teams, plus same-day repeat-reading audit reasons.Dependency-Aware Doctor Queue: Separates patients into Ready for Visit (temperature recorded) and Blocked — Waiting on Nurse (with a Ping nurse button and documented emergency override option).3-Day Fever-Free Discharge Workflow: Automatically tracks consecutive fever-free days, requires a Doctor's discharge recommendation, and routes to Administrator sign-off to release the bed.Outcomes & Mortality Benchmark: Tracks recoveries and per-patient mortality (releasing beds on death) against the ≤ 15% mortality benchmark, with a configurable fever threshold.Judge / Demo Controls: Prev Day (⏮) and Next Day (⏭) buttons to step through multi-day fever-free streaks, plus Reset demo data (↺) to restore the 74-patient starting state.Demo Login CredentialsUse the 1-click sign-in buttons on the login screen or
